Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Thank you for using TransViewer (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information.

1. About the App

TransViewer is a locally-operated translation tool:

  • The App itself does not provide translation services
  • The App does not operate any servers
  • All features run locally on your device by default

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or upload any user data.

This includes but is not limited to:

  • No personal identity information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • No device information (device ID, IP address, etc.)
  • No usage behavior or log data

3. Third-Party Services (User-Configured)

The App allows users to configure their own third-party translation API Keys (e.g., third-party translation platforms).

  • These third-party services are enabled solely at the user's discretion
  • When you use a third-party service, your request data (such as input text) may be sent to the respective third-party provider
  • The collection, processing, and storage of data is governed by that third party's privacy policy
We do not access, store, or upload your API Keys, and we do not log your translation content.

4. Local Data Storage

  • All data (such as settings, API Keys, and local records) is stored only on your device
  • We have no ability to access or retrieve this data

5. Network Requests

  • The App does not proactively connect to any developer servers
  • Any network requests occur only when you enable a third-party translation service
  • These requests are handled directly by the third-party service you choose

6. Third-Party Disclaimer

  • We do not control the behavior of third-party services
  • We are not responsible for how they handle your data
  • We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party service you use

7. Permissions

The App only requests permissions necessary for its core functionality and will not request permissions unrelated to its features.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not actively collect any information and does not target children for data collection.

9. Policy Changes

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will update the notice within the App.
